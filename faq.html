Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about my website services, pricing, and process.

Pricing

How much does a website cost?
Pricing starts at $300 for small websites and $400+ for multi-page websites. Final pricing depends on features and customization.

Do I have to pay everything upfront?
A non-refundable 50% deposit is required upfront to begin the project, with the remaining balance due upon completion before final delivery.
The deposit secures your project slot and covers initial design and development work.

Process

How does the process work?
After discussing your needs, I design and build your website, share progress updates, and make revisions before final delivery.

Can I request changes?
Yes, revisions are included depending on the service selected.

Timeline

How long does it take to built a website?
Most websites take 1-3 weeks depending on size, features, and overall complexity. Timelines may vary based on how quickly content and feedback are provided.

Availability

When can you start my project?
Project start dates depend on current availability. Projects are scheduled and completed one at a time to ensure focused attention and quality results. Booking in advance is recommended.

Features

Can my website be mobile friendly?
Yes, all websites are designed to be fully responsive across phones, tablets, and desktops. If preferred, a mobile-only design can be created for projects where desktop optimization is not required.

Bilingual

Do you offer bilingual websites?
Yes, I can build websites in English and Spanish.

Communication

How do we communicate during the project?
We can communicate via text, email, or your preferred method.
